Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how courses, certifications, and mentorship work? Here are our straightforward answers.

Do you offer accredited certificates upon course completion? +
Yes, absolutely! Each completed course grants a verifiable digital certificate with a unique verification link that you can share on LinkedIn, embed in your resume, or share with employers.
How do the 1-on-1 mentor review sessions work? +
Mentors provide async video reviews and written notes on your submitted capstone assignments within 48 hours. Lifetime members also receive scheduled live video consultations for in-depth career strategy and code architecture review.
Can I switch plans or request a refund if I'm not satisfied? +
Yes. We offer a no-questions-asked 14-day money-back guarantee on all our plans. Monthly subscribers can cancel at any point with one click right from their account dashboard.
Are these courses beginner-friendly? +
Yes! Each track begins with clear foundational concepts before advancing step-by-step into modern frameworks. Every lesson contains downloadable starter code, design templates, and step-by-step guidance.
Can I study at my own pace around a busy full-time job? +
All courses are 100% self-paced with 24/7 access on web and mobile devices. You can pause, rewatch, and submit assignments whenever your schedule permits without missing deadlines.
